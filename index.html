01Information we collect

None. MergeBox does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, advertising ID, usage statistics or crash reports. This version contains no advertising, analytics or tracking libraries.

02Your files

  • All merging, splitting, compressing, page numbering, watermarking and password protection happen on your device. Your files and their content are never uploaded.
  • MergeBox opens only the files and folders you choose through the Android system picker or the share menu. It never asks for access to all files on your device.
  • Exported files are saved to the folder you choose. From the Library screen, MergeBox can rename or delete only files that it exported itself, and only when you ask it to.
  • If you pick a file stored with a cloud provider, such as Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox, Android and that provider deliver it to MergeBox. That transfer is covered by the provider's own privacy policy. MergeBox itself sends nothing to them.

Your originals are read-only. MergeBox never modifies, moves, renames or deletes the files you open.

03Data stored on your device

To work, MergeBox keeps the following data in its private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read it.

Library
A list of the files you exported: name, location, page count, size and date.
Trash
A copy of each file you delete from the Library, kept for 30 days so you can restore it, then removed automatically.
Camera photos
Photos you take with the in-app camera, used to build your PDF.
Thumbnails
Page previews, which you can clear in Settings.
Settings
Your language, theme, default folder and export preferences.

All of this data is removed when you uninstall the app.

04Android backup

If you have turned on Google backup on your device, Android may include MergeBox's private data in your device backup, which is stored in your Google account. Google handles this under its own privacy policy, and you can turn it off in your device settings. MergeBox has no access to these backups.

05Permissions

PermissionWhy it is used

MergeBox does not request the "All files access" permission. Android libraries in the app list the "network state" permission, but MergeBox has no internet permission and does not connect to the internet.

06Children

MergeBox is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, including children.

07Security

Your files never leave your device through MergeBox, so the app does not expose them to network transfer. Password protection uses AES encryption applied on your device.

08Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, for example by adding optional features that need the internet, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released. We will also update the Data Safety section on Google Play.
