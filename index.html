Summary MergeBox works entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. It does not upload your files anywhere. Information we collect None. MergeBox does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, advertising ID, usage statistics or crash reports. This version contains no advertising, analytics or tracking libraries. Your files All merging, splitting, compressing, page numbering, watermarking and password protection happen on your device. Your files and their content are never uploaded. MergeBox opens only the files and folders you choose through the Android system picker or the share menu. It never asks for access to all files on your device. Your original files are only read. MergeBox never modifies, moves, renames or deletes them. Exported files are saved to the folder you choose. From the Library screen, MergeBox can rename or delete only files that it exported itself, and only when you ask it to. If you pick a file stored with a cloud provider, such as Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox, Android and that provider deliver it to MergeBox. That transfer is covered by the provider's own privacy policy. MergeBox itself sends nothing to them. Data stored on your device To work, MergeBox keeps the following data in its private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read it. Library: a list of the files you exported (name, location, page count, size, date). Trash: a copy of each file you delete from the Library, kept for 30 days so you can restore it, then removed automatically. Camera photos: photos you take with the in-app camera, used to build your PDF. Thumbnails: page previews, which you can clear in Settings. Settings: your language, theme, default folder and export preferences. All of this data is removed when you uninstall the app. Android backup If you have turned on Google backup on your device, Android may include MergeBox's private data in your device backup, which is stored in your Google account. Google handles this under its own privacy policy, and you can turn it off in your device settings. MergeBox has no access to these backups. Permissions Permission Why it is used Camera Only while you take photos of pages to turn them into a PDF. Photos stay on your device. Notifications To show export progress and let you know when a long export is finished. Foreground service To keep a long export running safely while you switch to another app. MergeBox does not request the "All files access" permission. Android libraries in the app list the "network state" permission, but MergeBox has no internet permission and does not connect to the internet. Children MergeBox is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, including children. Security Your files never leave your device through MergeBox, so they are not exposed to network transfer by the app. Password protection uses AES encryption applied on your device. Changes to this policy If a future version changes how data is handled, for example by adding optional features that need the internet, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released. We will also update the Data Safety section on Google Play.
